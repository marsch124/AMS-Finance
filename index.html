How this works

What this app is

The AMS Finance Hub is the home screen for the monthly finance reporting. It does not hold any money data of its own — it looks at the real files (the budget workbook, the Obsidian reports, the ledger, the dashboard data) and shows you, at a glance, where everything stands and where everything lives.

It runs entirely on this Mac. A tiny engine (a local server on port 7780) reads the files and serves this page. Nothing is sent anywhere — closing the browser tab loses nothing, and reopening it shows the current truth again.

The monthly status board

One row per month since March 2026, when the reporting system started. The four columns are four independent checks against the real files:

  • Report — the file YYYY-MM-financial-report.md exists in the Obsidian Reports folder.
  • Ledger — the month has its row in monthly-ledger.csv.
  • Workbook — the month's actuals column (J–U) in the budget Excel actually contains numbers. The hub opens the workbook file and looks at the 16 category rows itself.
  • Dashboard — the month is included in the AMS Monthly Spend dashboard's data.

The Status pill sums it up: Complete (teal) = all four green. k of 4 (amber) = partially done. Not run yet (grey outline) = the current month, which is normal until the month ends. When a month ends, its report first shows Due [date] (grey — "bank statements settling"): the bank needs about a week before the export is complete, so nothing is expected of you yet. From the due day — the 8th unless you change it — it turns solid amber: Due — run this month. That's your cue to act.

Clicking any month that has a report opens that report in Obsidian.

Example
March 2026 shows a red cross under Workbook and "3 of 4": its report and ledger row exist, but its actuals were never typed into the workbook. The board will keep saying so until they are — the hub never assumes, it only reports what the files show.
The KPI cards

The four cards under the masthead show the latest reported month's headline numbers: income, expenses, net, and savings rate. They come from the same data file that feeds the AMS Monthly Spend dashboard, so they always match it.

  • Net = income − expenses. Green when positive, red when negative.
  • Savings rate = net ÷ income, as a percentage.
Example
"July 2026 · Net +29 665" means July's report counted 92 398 SEK of income (LÖN rows only) and 62 733 SEK of expenses, so 32 % of income was kept. When the August report is run, all four cards switch to August automatically.
Dashboards and the "months behind" badge

The two dashboard tiles open the published web dashboards. Each tile also admits when its dashboard is stale:

  • AMS Monthly Spend is compared against the newest report. If a report exists that the dashboard doesn't include yet, an amber badge counts the gap.
  • AMS Main Finance Dashboard is compared against the workbook. If the workbook holds newer actuals than the dashboard shows, the badge counts those months.
Example
"Refreshed 2026-08-27 · through May 2026 2 months behind" means the workbook already has June and July actuals, but the dashboard was last built from May. Refreshing the dashboard (ask Sam) clears the badge on the next reload.
The monthly checklist

The panel on the right is your working checklist for the month. Items are numbered in order, because the routine is a sequence. Everything is saved instantly to a small file next to the app, so it survives restarts and works in any browser.

  • Tick / untick — click the box. Ticked items fade to grey (no strikethrough).
  • Add — type in the field at the bottom and press Enter (or click +).
  • Edit — double-click the text, or hover the row and click the pencil.
  • Delete — hover the row, click the bin.
  • Reorder — hover the row, use the ▲ ▼ arrows. Numbers follow automatically.

The reset: the checklist unticks itself when a new period starts — by default on the 1st of each month. Click the settings symbol (two lines with dots) to choose another day (1–28) or to untick everything right now. Items are never deleted by a reset, only the ticks. The same settings panel also holds the board's "reports become due on day …" setting (default the 8th — bank statements need time to settle before the export is complete).

Self-ticking items ⚡: an item with a small lightning mark is linked to the status board. During a period you process the previous month, so when the hub sees that month's check go green, the item ticks itself. It happens at most once per month — if you untick it by hand afterwards, it stays unticked. Hover the lightning for a reminder of this.

Example
You run the July report with Sam. Next time the hub loads, it sees July's Report check is green and ticks "Run the monthly report with Sam" for you. "Refresh AMS Main Finance Dashboard" stays open, because the hub can see that dashboard is still behind the workbook — the checklist and the status board are one connected machine.
Shortcut tiles and their colours

Every tile is a one-click opener, grouped in colour families:

  • Dashboards (teal) — the two published web dashboards.
  • Budget files (gold) — the budget workbook 2026–2038, the weekly follow-up Excel, and the monthly ledger. Opens in your normal spreadsheet app.
  • Obsidian notes (violet) — the latest report, the manual expenses log, the wealth log, the canonical state note, and the report runbook. Opens in Obsidian.
  • Folders (blue) — Budgeting, Reports 2026, and Bank imports. Opens in Finder.

The icons are drawn by hand as sketchy ink lines — no stock symbols.

Storage badges — where each thing lives

The small symbol in the top-right corner of every panel says where that data is actually stored:

  • Cloud — iCloud  ·  Open box — Dropbox  ·  Triangle — Google Drive  ·  Globe — the web (claude.ai)
  • Laptop — this MacBook Pro  ·  Screen — AMS Home (the Mac at home)  ·  Phone — iPhone  ·  ? — other

Click any badge to change it. A small menu opens with the synced services on top and the devices below — pick the right one and it's saved instantly (locally, in the app's own folder, like the checklist). Hover a badge to read its current setting in words.

Example
The two dashboards start with the globe (they live on claude.ai), and everything else starts as this MacBook Pro. If you move the budget workbook into Dropbox one day, click its badge and choose the box — the hub doesn't move any files, it just records what you tell it.
The engine under the hood

A small local server (the "engine") on port 7780 does the reading: it checks the report files, parses the ledger, opens the Excel workbook, and reads the dashboard data. This page is just its face.

  • At login the engine starts by itself, silently — the hub is always ready.
  • AMS Finance.app (double-click, or from the Dock) opens the hub page, starting the engine first if needed.
  • The engine writes its diary to ~/Library/Logs/AMS-Finance.log.

If the page won't load: double-click AMS Finance.app — that heals it. If a macOS question about the Documents folder ever appears (for example after a system update), click Allow; the hub needs it to read the budget workbook.

A month in the life (worked example)

Here is how a full cycle looks, using September 2026 as the stage:

  1. Sept 1, morning. The checklist unticks itself (new period). August's row shows a grey Due 8 Sep — the month is over, but the bank statements are still settling, so the hub isn't nagging yet. On the 8th it turns amber: Due — run this month. The KPI cards still show July — they always show the newest finished report.
  2. Export. You download August's transactions from Swedbank and tick item 1 by hand. A cash expense you dictated during August is already in the expenses log — tick item 2.
  3. Run the report. You ask Sam to run the August report. When it's done, the report file and the ledger row exist. Next time the hub loads, August's Report and Ledger turn green and "Run the monthly report with Sam" ⚡ ticks itself.
  4. Resolve. Flagged merchants and ticket purchases were decided during the run — tick item 4 yourself (that judgment is yours, not a file the hub can check).
  5. Workbook. Sam writes the actuals into column R. The board's Workbook check goes green; the checklist item ⚡ ticks itself; the Main Finance Dashboard tile now says 1 month behind — it hasn't caught up with the new column yet.
  6. Dashboards. Sam refreshes both dashboards. The badges disappear, August's Dashboard check turns green, the row flips to teal Complete, both ⚡ items tick, and the KPI cards switch to August's numbers.
  7. Done. 7 of 7 ticked, every row green. Until October 1st, when the wheel turns again.
Abbreviations and definitions
AMS
Anna & Martin Schabbauer — the household. The prefix all your apps share.
Actuals
What was really spent in a month, per category — as opposed to budget, which is the plan. Written into the workbook's monthly columns.
Budget workbook
"Martin & Anna Budget 2026-2038.xlsx" — the master Excel file. Column H holds the monthly budget; columns J–U hold each month's actuals.
Dashboard
A published web page with charts. AMS Monthly Spend shows a month's spending in detail; AMS Main Finance Dashboard shows budget, net worth and the 2026–2038 runway.
Due
A month that has ended but whose report hasn't been run yet. It counts as due from the due day of the following month (default the 8th); before that it shows "bank statements settling".
Engine
The small local server (port 7780) that reads your files and serves this page.
KPI
Key Performance Indicator — a headline number worth watching. Here: income, expenses, net, savings rate.
Ledger
monthly-ledger.csv — one row per month and category with actual vs budget. The long-term memory of the reporting system.
LÖN
Swedish for salary. Income in the reports counts LÖN rows only.
Net
Income minus expenses for a month. Positive = money kept.
Period
The checklist's month, running from reset day to reset day. During a period you process the previous calendar month.
Report
The monthly financial report — a 16-section Markdown document in Obsidian, produced by the monthly-financial-report skill.
Reset day
The day of the month (1–28) when the checklist unticks itself. Default: the 1st.
Sam
Your robot assistant in Obsidian, who runs the monthly report, writes the workbook, and refreshes the dashboards.
Savings rate
Net ÷ income, in percent. How much of what came in stayed.
SEK
Swedish krona — all amounts in this hub.
Staleness badge
The amber "X months behind" mark on a dashboard tile whose dashboard lags the newest data.
Storage badge
The small symbol in a panel's top-right corner showing where that data lives (iCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive, web, MacBook Pro, AMS Home, iPhone). Click it to change; it records, it never moves files.
⚡ Self-ticking item
A checklist item linked to a status check; it ticks itself when the hub sees the work is done.
